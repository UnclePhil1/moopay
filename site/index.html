How it works

Three easy steps

You don't need to learn anything new. Your AI assistant does the work.

1

Ask your AI

Say who you want to pay, how much, and which network they use. For example: "Send 5 USDC to my friend's Ethereum wallet."

2

Pay from your wallet

Your AI gives you a link. Open it, pick USDC or USDT, press Send, and approve in your wallet (Meteor, Nightly or MyNearWallet). MooPay never holds your money.

3

They get paid

The money lands in their wallet on Bitcoin, Solana or Ethereum. They don't pay any fees to receive it.

Ways to send

Pick what fits

Send it now

You know where the money should go. Give your AI their wallet address and open the pay link it gives you. This is the simplest way.

Send a claim code

You don't know their wallet yet. Your AI makes a secret claim code and you share it. They choose where the money goes when they use it.

Get started

Add MooPay to your AI app

MooPay works with Claude and other AI apps that let you add tools (this is called MCP). It runs on your own computer.

  1. Install MooPay

    You need Node.js 20 or newer. Open a terminal in the MooPay folder and run this once.

    npm install && npm run build

  2. Tell us where MooPay is

    Paste the full path to the MooPay folder on your computer.

  3. Add it to your AI app

    Open Claude Desktop, go to Settings → Developer → Edit Config, and paste this. Then restart Claude.

Good to know

      
Please read this first

      

        
It's real money. There is no practice mode. Start with a small amount, like 2 or 3 USDC.

        
Keep claim codes safe. Anyone who has the code can take the money. If you lose it, the money is lost.

        
Not a perfect privacy tool. MooPay makes it harder to link the sender and the receiver, but it can't hide everything.

        
Small fees apply. There is a network fee of about 0.3 USDC on each payment, so tiny payments aren't worth it. Your AI shows you the price before you pay.
