---
title: "moo points"
description: "Earn moo points for every payment above $10 that your agent finishes."
---

moo points are a count of how much you use MooPay. A reward program will use them later. Nothing can be redeemed yet.

## How you earn

| Rule | Detail |
| :- | :- |
| Points per payment | 10 |
| Payments that count | Sends, claims and stakes above $10 that finish. |
| When they arrive | When the payment is delivered, claimed or staked. |

A payment of $10 or less earns nothing. A payment of $500 earns the same 10 points as one of $11.

## What does not count

- Payments that fail, are refunded, or are never claimed.
- Money you move to your own wallet, including your payout wallet.
- Sweeps, withdrawals and adding money.

## See your points

Your agent wallet card shows your total. The agent also tells you in chat each time you earn some.
