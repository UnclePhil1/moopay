## What is MooPay?

An AI money agent. You tell it in plain words to send, split, schedule or stake money, and it sends to Bitcoin, Solana, Ethereum and NEAR and takes money in from many other chains. See [What is MooPay](/docs/guides/get-started/what-is-moopay).

## Do I need to sign up?

No. MooPay makes a wallet for your browser. Save the recovery code so you can come back.

## Can I use it without the agent wallet?

Yes. When the agent wallet cannot cover a payment, you sign in your own wallet.

## Which chains work?

You can send to Bitcoin, Solana, Ethereum and NEAR. You can pay from many other chains too. See [Supported chains](/docs/guides/resources/supported-chains).

## Is it private?

Partly. Chat can run inside sealed enclaves and history is optional. On-chain payments are still public. Read [Privacy](/docs/guides/your-account/privacy).

## Is it safe?

It is new and not audited. Read [Safety and trust](/docs/guides/resources/safety-and-trust) for the honest details.

## I lost my recovery code

Without it, you cannot restore the account on a new browser. See [Recovery code](/docs/guides/your-account/recovery-code).

## Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The pay window opens as a sheet and lets you pick any wallet.
