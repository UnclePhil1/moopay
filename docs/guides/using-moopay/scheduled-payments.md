---
title: "Scheduled payments"
description: "Repeat a payment every day, week or month."
---

Say: `Send $10 to <address> on Solana every week`.

The agent saves the schedule. Each run follows your [rules](/docs/guides/your-account/rules-and-limits), so a run that goes over your limit asks first or stops.

## See and stop schedules

Say `Show my account` to list schedules with their ids. Say `Cancel schedule <id>` to stop one.

## Good to know

- Schedules need money in the agent wallet, because nobody is there to sign.
- Group payouts can repeat too. Add the word `every` and a period.
