---
title: "MooPay on X"
description: "Ask MooPay for things from X by mentioning it, and confirm in a reply."
---

You can use MooPay without opening the app. Link your X account once, then mention MooPay in a post and say what you want, like "send $10 to alice.near" or "stake $20".

## Link your account

1. In MooPay, type "link my X account". You get a one-time code that works for 15 minutes.
2. From your X account, post the line MooPay shows you, for example `@moopay link moo-ABC123`.
3. MooPay replies to confirm. Your account is linked.

To unlink, type "unlink my X account" in MooPay.

## Ask for things

Mention MooPay and say it in plain words. Send, split between people, schedule, stake, private claims and balance checks all work. If a person has linked their X account, you can use their @name as the receiver.

Before any money moves, MooPay replies with what it is about to do, the receiver, the amount and the fee. Reply "confirm" to go ahead. Anything else cancels it.

## Fee

Payments made on X carry a small service fee, shown in the summary before you confirm. The same payment in the app has no extra fee.

## Good to know

- Replies are public, so keep private details out of your posts. For anything sensitive, use the app.
- Your rules and limits apply on X exactly as in the app. Large payments still ask for your approval.
- Anyone who can post from your linked X account can ask MooPay to pay inside those limits, so link an account you keep safe.
