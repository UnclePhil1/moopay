---
title: "Group payments"
description: "Pay many people at once, or share one claim link with a group."
---

There are two ways to do a group.

| | Group payout | Group claim |
| :- | :- | :- |
| What it does | Sends to several wallets in one request. | Makes one link, and each named wallet opens its own part. |
| Chains | Mix any chains and assets. | NEAR, Solana and EVM wallets. |
| Approval | One OK covers the whole batch. | One OK covers the whole link. |

## Group payout

Say: `Send $10 in SOL to <address 1>, $5 in ETH to <address 2>, and $20 USDC to <address 3>`.

You can add 2 to 20 lines. Add `every week` to repeat the group on a schedule.

## Group claim

Say: `Split $60 between these wallets: <a>, <b>, <c>`.

You get one link to share. Each wallet can open only its own claim, for its own amount.
