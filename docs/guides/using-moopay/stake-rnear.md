title: "Stake rNEAR"
description: "Stake NEAR on Rhea Finance and earn yield, from any chain."

rNEAR is a liquid staking token from [Rhea Finance](https://rhea.finance). You stake NEAR, get rNEAR, and it earns yield. MooPay never holds your rNEAR. It goes to your own NEAR wallet.

## Stake

Say: `Stake $20 into rNEAR`. You can also pay from another chain, like `Stake 0.5 SOL`.

:::steps
1. A Stake window opens. Enter the NEAR wallet that should get your rNEAR.
2. Pick the wallet you want to pay with, and sign.
3. MooPay turns it into NEAR and stakes it.
4. The agent tells you when staking is done.
:::

:::note
Staking needs a little NEAR in your agent wallet for the network fee. Keep about $1 worth there. If the fee is low, the stake waits, and MooPay may lend a small advance and take it back after.
:::

## Unstake

Say: `Unstake my rNEAR`. A window opens and you sign with the wallet that holds it. You can cancel before you sign.

After a day or two the NEAR is ready. Say `Withdraw my unstaked NEAR` and sign again.

## Check your position

Say `Show my rNEAR`. You see the amount, its value, the yield, and anything unstaking.

## Risks

Staking has smart contract risk and the yield changes. This is not financial advice.
