---
title: "Fees"
description: "See what you pay when you use MooPay."
---

MooPay adds a service fee of 1% in the chat and 2% on X. It applies to sends, claims and stakes paid from your agent wallet, is shown in the summary before you confirm, and is taken once the payment lands. Payments you sign in your own wallet carry no service fee. The other fees come from the networks and services MooPay uses.

| Fee | When | Who gets it |
| :- | :- | :- |
| MooPay service fee | Sends, claims and stakes from the agent wallet. 1% in the chat, 2% on X. | MooPay. |
| Swap fee | Cross-chain payments through 1Click. | The swap service. |
| Network fee | Every on-chain transfer. | The network. |
| Staking reserve | Staking window holds about 0.15 NEAR for fees. | Network, and the rest is returned. |

## Gas on NEAR

Some steps need a little NEAR. If your agent wallet has none, MooPay can lend a small advance and take it back after. If that is off, keep about $1 of NEAR in the agent wallet.

The quote you see before you sign shows the amount the receiver gets and what you pay.
