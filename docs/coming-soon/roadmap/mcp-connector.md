MCP lets AI apps use outside tools. With the connector, you could ask Claude or ChatGPT to pay someone, and the payment would run through your MooPay rules.

## What to expect

- Connect once with a private link.
- The same limits as the chat apply.
- Payments you must approve still wait for your OK.

## Today

You can already use the [agent API](/docs/developers/agent-api/agent-keys) with a key.
